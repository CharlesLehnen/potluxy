About Potluxy

Potluxy is 100% free and doesn’t require logins for guests. Just share your event link and let everyone choose what they’re bringing.

Whenever you need multiple people to bring different items to an event, think Potluxy. Whether you’re planning a dinner party, organizing a festive Friendsgiving, or coordinating a camping trip, Potluxy makes it simple.

How it works

  1. Create an event. Just a name is enough. Date, location, and Partiful or when2meet links are all optional.
  2. List what you need. Type items one by one, paste a whole list, or import a CSV (export the template first).
  3. Share one link. Guests tap “I’ll bring” next to an item. No sign-up, no app.
  4. See who’s bringing what. Gaps are highlighted, so nothing gets missed and nothing gets doubled.

Perfect for

  • Holiday gatherings — Thanksgiving, Friendsgiving, Christmas, Hanukkah, Diwali, Eid, Lunar New Year, and more
  • Celebrations — birthdays, anniversaries, graduations, baby showers
  • Dinner parties — Seder dinners, potlucks, cookouts, barbecues
  • Game nights — board games, LAN parties, Super Bowl watch parties
  • Outdoor events — picnics, beach days, camping trips, tailgates
  • Office & community — team lunches, block parties, family reunions

Privacy

No accounts. Guests type a name, and that’s it. Emails are only collected if the host asks for reminders, and only the host can see them. Guests never see each other’s emails, and passwords are optional and stored hashed. Potluxy is source-available, so you can read exactly how your data is handled.
