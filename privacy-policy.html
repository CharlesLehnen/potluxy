Privacy Policy

Introduction

At Potluxy, we are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

Information We Collect

We collect the following data when you use our platform:

  • Account Information: Event-specific username and password for access. Each event has its own unique user profile, separate from other events.
  • Event Details: Event name, description, date, and items required for planning.
  • Commitments: Items you commit to bring for a specific event.

Information We Do Not Collect

We prioritize your privacy and only collect essential data for event management. We do not collect or store the following:

  • Email Addresses: Our system operates on username-based event profiles without requiring personal email addresses.
  • Cross-Event User Tracking: Your username is specific to each event and is not linked across multiple events.
  • Personal Identifiable Information (PII): We do not collect birthdates, phone numbers, addresses, or other sensitive personal details.
  • Marketing Data: We do not share user data for advertising or marketing purposes.

Third-Party Advertising & Cookies

We use third-party vendors, including Google, to serve ads on Potluxy. These vendors may use cookies or web beacons to personalize ads based on your prior visits to this website or other sites on the internet.

Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to Potluxy and/or other sites on the web. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Ad Settings.

You can also review Google’s advertising policies and how they use data here.

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used for the following purposes:

  • To manage and display events you create or join.
  • To track items and commitments for each event.
  • To improve our platform and user experience.

Data Security

We use industry-standard encryption and row-level security (RLS) to protect your data, as outlined in our database schema and policies.

Your Rights

You have the right to access, update, or delete your account and event-related data at any time. Please contact us for assistance.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about our privacy practices, please contact us at support@potluxy.com.
